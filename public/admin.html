The UWANI platform utilizes an embedded SQLite3 relational database engine hosted at database/uwani.db. It is managed by a Node.js Express application offering RESTful endpoints with schema integrity, foreign keys, and indexes. All modifications reflect across both the customer storefront and the administrative suite.
