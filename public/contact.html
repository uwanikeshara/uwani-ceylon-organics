Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding our sustainable supply chain, product grading, and islandwide delivery.

What distinguishes True Ceylon Cinnamon (Alba) from supermarket cinnamon?
Over 90% of global cinnamon sold in standard supermarkets is actually Cassia (Cinnamomum cassia), which contains dangerously high levels of coumarin (a natural compound harmful to liver health in regular doses). UWANI harvests only pure Cinnamomum verum (True Ceylon Cinnamon) from southern Sri Lanka. Alba is the highest recognized grade—pencil-thin, fragrant, naturally sweet, with less than 0.004% trace coumarin.
How are fragile terracotta pottery and loose leaf teas packaged for delivery?
We utilize 100% biodegradable corrugated cardboard cushions, shredded recycled paper pads, and natural jute coir ties. Our terracotta clay mugs are nested securely in molded cellulose pulp trays, completely avoiding synthetic bubble wrap or styrofoam while ensuring zero transit breakage.
Do you support wholesale supply for specialty cafes and overseas retailers?
Yes. We supply boutique tea houses, organic restaurants, and wellness studios in Sri Lanka, Singapore, Australia, and the UK. Bulk orders from 5kg to 500kg come with organic certificates of analysis and phytosanitary documentation. Contact us using the form above for our trade catalog.
Where is the academic development and database host located?
This web application was created for the Web Application Development module at NSBM Green University Town (Pitipana, Homagama). The backend is powered by Node.js, Express, and an embedded SQLite database demonstrating full DML (Insert, Select, Update, Delete) architecture and GitHub version control.
