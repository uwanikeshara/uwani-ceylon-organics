Ethical Agroforestry

Rooted in Ceylon's Heritage, Nurtured with Respect

UWANI was conceived at the intersection of traditional Sri Lankan agro-wisdom and modern circular sustainability. We collaborate directly with over 40 generational farming families across Nuwara Eliya, Matale, and Kegalle.

By eliminating commercial intermediaries and ensuring guaranteed above-market living wages, we empower local smallholders to preserve ancestral forest farming methods without synthetic chemicals or soil degradation.
